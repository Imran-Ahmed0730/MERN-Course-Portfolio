About me

Turning ideas into clean, usable interfaces.

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering student at the University of Dhaka. What started as curiosity about how websites work turned into a habit of building something new every week.

Today I focus on front-end development with a strong eye for responsive layouts, readable code, and the small details that make an interface feel effortless. I'm working towards becoming a full-stack MERN developer and collaborating with teams that care about good products.

Outside of code, I enjoy sketching interface ideas, following design trends, and playing football with friends — then bringing the best ideas back into my next build.

  • Build accessible, mobile-first websites
  • Grow into a full-stack MERN engineer
  • Contribute to open-source projects
  • Keep learning something new every week
